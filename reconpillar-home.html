Platform Strengths

Everything Your Jobsite Needs. Nothing It Doesn't.

⚡

Plug-and-Play in Minutes

Mount with a magnet, strap, or 2×4 slot. Any crew member deploys a sensor in under five minutes — no networking, no configuration, no IT ticket.

📡

Cellular, Not Site Wi-Fi

Pillar Smart Pods run on 915 MHz ISM radio and connect to the cloud over cellular — so they work in basements, shafts, and dead zones where Wi-Fi never reaches.

🔋

18-Month Battery Life

Non-rechargeable Lithium Thionyl Chloride battery rated for 18 months. Deploy on day one and don't think about batteries until the project is done.

🔔

Instant Text & Email Alerts

Set thresholds, route alerts by role and zone. The superintendent gets the leak on their floor; safety gets the CO spike. No forwarding required.

📄

Automatic Paper Trail

Every reading logged with a timestamp. Compliance reports and closeout packages generate themselves — ready for owners, inspectors, and insurance.

👥

Unlimited Users

Every stakeholder — GC, sub, owner, safety manager, inspector — gets access at no extra cost. No seat fees, no user caps, ever.
